What the homeowner never has to think about

Every screen is driven by the on-device engines. The phone is only a window — pull the internet and the Core keeps deciding.

Engine 1 · Predictive health

Coulomb counting, OCV and an internal-resistance probe are fused by a Kalman filter, then a tiny quantised model maps the trend to SoH and an RUL window.

Engine 2 · Blackout autopilot

Robust rule-based tiers made smarter by a learned demand-and-outage forecaster. Pre-charges before a predicted cut; sheds through external contactors.

Engine 3 · Coach & Grid Shield

Low-frequency load disaggregation for headline appliances, plus one-cycle RMS sag/swell detection logged for appliance-protection analytics.

Engine 4 · Fleet intelligence

Anonymised telemetry sharpens the shared models centrally, with optional privacy-preserving federated learning for connected homes.
