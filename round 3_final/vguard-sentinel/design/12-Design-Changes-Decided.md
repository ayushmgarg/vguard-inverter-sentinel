# 12 — Design Changes Decided (consolidated)

Every change below was forced by working a report claim out to a T. Each is stated as **what the report says → what is true → the decision → what to say at the finale**. The submitted report is frozen; these are the positions the team defends in Q&A and carries into the Phase-4 deck.

| # | Report says | Finding | Decision | What to say |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **D1** | Energy Coach does NILM "from the ≤1 Hz current signal" (§8); basic BOM senses battery DC only | Battery DC current carries no load information when the grid is present; NILM needs P/Q on the inverter's **AC output** (06 §1.1). The report as written cannot deliver the Coach. | **Add a metering AFE — ATM90E32AS — with a CT on the inverter AC output (and an optional mains CT).** BOM +₹600–1,100. | "The Coach reads real and reactive power at the inverter output through a metering front end — the same class of chip inside a smart meter — not the battery shunt." |
| **D2** | "Adaptive charge-and-thermal policy extends battery life 15–30 %" (§3, §15), retrofit and embedded alike | Indian home inverters use analog SG3525/TL494 charge loops with no exposed control path (03 §0). Retrofit cannot command the charger. | **Split into two SKUs — Sentinel-Embedded (DAC/UART charger control, hardware safety ceiling) and Sentinel-Retrofit (advisory + load-side protection only).** Life-extension claim scoped to Embedded and to be **measured** in the campaign (04 §4). | "On new V-Guard inverters we control the charger; on retrofit we advise and protect from the load side. We claim life extension only where we control charging, and we measure it." |
| **D3** | SoC re-anchored to OCV "during float-rest" (§5.2, §6.1) | Float voltage is charger-regulated, not open-circuit; this over-reports SoC (01 §0). | **Replace with two mechanisms: full detection by charge-current taper (charger ON) and rest-OCV only with the charger verifiably OFF, two-tier by rest time.** | "We anchor at 100 % on current taper, like a Victron shunt monitor, and we only use the OCV table after a genuine rest with the charger off." |
| **D4** | Sampling "128–256 samples/cycle" on the ESP32-S3 (Exh. 14) | The S3's SAR ADC is not metering-grade (INL ±8 LSB, non-linear, no synchronous V/I) (06 §1.4). | **Metering on the AFE (D1); the ESP32 ADC keeps only the AMC1311 fast half-cycle-RMS/outage tap.** ATM90E36A pin-compatible if on-chip THD is wanted. | "Metrology lives in a dedicated AFE; the microcontroller only does the fast sag/outage sensing where 10 %-class accuracy is enough." |
| **D5** | "Prioritising inside the single essential circuit" (§7) | One contactor per tier cannot re-prioritise loads inside it (05 §5). | **Reword to staged reserve alerts + advisory + optional installer sub-circuit; smart-plug integration is roadmap.** | "Granularity equals the number of contactor channels wired. Inside one circuit we alert and advise; we don't pretend to switch what we can't." |
| **D6** | Federated learning improves "the models" — implicitly SoH/RUL (§9) | SoH has no on-device labels; the habit forecaster is an EWMA table, not a network (08 §0). | **Federate the NILM appliance classifier (user-labelled, privacy-sensitive) and optionally the anomaly AE; SoH/RUL stays centrally trained on campaign + telemetry with on-device conformal personalisation. DP-FTRL + SecAgg+ + FedProx; ε ≈ 4 with stated cost.** | "We federate the model that actually has private labels on the device — which appliances you own. Battery health is learned from the fleet's pooled, consented, pseudonymised summaries." |
| **D7** | Basic board ~₹500 / embedded incremental ₹150–300 (§10) | AFE, DS3231, ATECC608, DAC, supervisory timer are required for the claims made (10 §9). | **Restate by engine tier: basic board (Engines 1+2, with RTC/secure element/supervisory timer) ≈ ₹750, ≈ ₹1,050 standalone with shunt; Coach kit (Engine 3: AFE + CT + tap + isolator) +₹600–1,000 → full retrofit ≈ ₹1,650–2,050; embedded increment ₹450–700 basic / ₹850–1,300 with Coach. Contactors extra.** | "Still a small fraction of a ₹15–18 k battery; the metering front end alone unlocks the Coach and the Grid Shield evidence." |
| **D8** | "Fail-safe: loads default to energised" (§17) — mechanism unspecified | Needs NC contactors, coil economiser, pull-downs, an independent supervisory timer, and a hardware medical jumper (10 §4). | **Adopt all five; latching relays explicitly rejected.** | "Coil off means load on. Kill the processor and every load comes back within two seconds — we demo it." |
| **D9** | "Microamp quiescent" (§5.3) | Realistic average ≈ 2.6 mA battery-side (≈ 0.04 %/day); NILM-on adds ~4 mA; shedding adds coil hold (10 §5). | **Publish the budget as a proposal to be measured; outage policy = Wi-Fi off, BLE 5 s, NILM off.** | "Milliamps, not microamps — still under a twentieth of the battery's own self-discharge." |
| **D10** | Time-of-day learning (§7) with no clock provision | ESP32 RTC drifts unpowered (10 §6). | **Add DS3231 + CR2032.** | — |
| **D11** | "Signed, device-key-authenticated health log" (§3) with no key store | Needs a secure element for a non-extractable key (09 §4). | **Add ATECC608; ECDSA-P256 hash-chained log; eFuse-only as low-cost fallback.** | "The log proves what the device saw; the key never leaves the chip." |
| **D12** | Uncertainty method for RUL unspecified (§6.3) | MC-dropout impractical on TFLM (02 §2.4). | **Quantile heads + 3-seed ensemble + split-conformal offsets; window, never a date; N weeks = P10.** | "Six to fourteen weeks, plan for six — and that band is calibrated on held-out batteries." |
| **D13** | Outage prediction (§7) | Only scheduled patterns are predictable from one home's history (05 §2). | **Pre-charge is active on Embedded, advisory on Retrofit; always reported with confidence; must beat climatology in the pilot or ships advisory-only.** | "We forecast the roster, not the storm." |
| **D14** | Adaptive-charging "thermal profile" | Adds current derating > 45 °C, hard stop ≈ 58 °C, equalisation gated on sulphation signatures and forecast (03 §3.2). | Adopt as the policy loop; water-loss guard on equalisation hours. | — |
| **D15** | §11 promises a Flower simulation across ≥5 simulated homes with Secure Aggregation and DP | Federated learning is deferred by the team (2026-09-21); design 08 §B5 fully specifies the simulation for later. | **Cut from the prototype scope; not demonstrated at the finale.** Keep 08 as the design of record; say "designed, not built". | "Fleet learning today is central training on consented, pseudonymised summaries; the federated layer is specified but deliberately not in this prototype." |
| **D16** | "Neural accelerator" (exec-summary/draft wording) and "microamp quiescent draw" (§5.3) | The ESP32-S3 has vector/SIMD extensions used by ESP-NN, not an NPU; realistic draw is ≈2–3 mA average (D9). | **Terminology fixed everywhere: "vector/SIMD extensions (ESP-NN)"; "milliamp-class average draw".** | — |
| **D17** | EKF §9 pseudocode applies a voltage correction every tick | Building it showed the per-tick correction re-creates the float≠OCV bug during charging (01 §11). | **Gate the generic voltage update to charger-off; while charging use coulomb counting + the two explicit anchors.** | "We fixed this twice: once on paper, once when the code told us the paper was still wrong." |

## Items the report got right and should be defended as-is
Tubular lead-acid focus and dimensions; EoL = 80 % SoH; Arrhenius halving per +10 °C; external shunt + INA228 (mV only on the PCB); 2-of-3 outage detection; NC contactors; IEEE 1159 / IEC 61000-4-30 Class-S-like framing; Flower + DP-FTRL + SecAgg; roadmap as validation gates; warranty ≈ 1.5 % of revenue as the business lever; IS/IEC 62368-1 as the BIS gate.
